<h1> Hard Boiled 🥚</h1>
- Welcome to Hard Boiled where you have to kill rotten eggs in this roguelite prototype emulating Brotato.
- Hardened in the Egg-o-pocalypse where egg's brains got scrambled and turned rotten, you gotta kill them before they kill you. 
- This is game is a prototype and may be updated with further features.
<h2> Features </h2>
- WASD for movement and spacebar (or shift) to dash. Attacking is automatic.
- Play as a Hard Boiled Egg with 20 health. How many waves can you last?
- Enemies include: 
 Slow Boiled: Slow and easy to kill. A little slow in the head and in the feet.
 Medium boiled: Wears a skull hat. Cosmetic or for threatening? No one knows.
 Fast boiled: Drank too much coffee. Speeds too fast.
 Shooter: A turret and an egg, shoots three eggs in an arc. Difficult and will drain your health if you don't kill it fast
 Chaser: Leaps at you in a rage. Has anger issues. But gets blocked by other enemies so isn't too difficult.
- Your weapons: 3 mini axes, a laser gun and a pistol. You should've gotten better supplies, maybe next time. Oh you also have one human fist for some reason, God knows where you got it from but desperate times call for desperate measures.
- Waves: 20 second waves. Enemies swarm you. Dodge and dash and try to survive for as long as you can.
- Good luck and don't get scrambled.

Ai was used for debugging occassionally and not used for this README or itch description
